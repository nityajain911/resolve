# Validation notes

## Core interview question

> **"Walk me through your last five overdue invoices. Why was each late, and where did you discover that reason?"**

Follow-ups: How many reminders went out before you knew? Who found out (accounts, sales,
dispatch)? Did a reminder ever go to a buyer who had already paid or was disputing? What would
you never let software do without your approval?
